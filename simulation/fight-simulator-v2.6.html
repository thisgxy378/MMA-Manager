Articulated joints

New: Visible shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle and neck joints follow the animated skeleton. The head tilts independently and knockdowns flex the limbs as the fighter falls.

Movement mechanics

Fixed limb lengths: Upper arms, forearms, thighs and shins retain their original lengths throughout every animation. Strikes must use joint rotation and positioning rather than stretching.

Jab: lead shoulder extends, elbow straightens, rear hand protects the chin, then the fist snaps back.

Lead hook: the elbow stays bent and raised while the glove sweeps horizontally across the opponent, driven by torso rotation rather than an extended arm.

Kick: rear knee chambers, support foot pivots, hips turn and the shin swings across before recovery.
